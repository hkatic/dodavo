## English (en-US)

**App name:** Dodavo

**Short description** (80 characters at most):
Send files and folders straight to another device, end-to-end encrypted.

**Full description:**

Dodavo sends files and folders directly from one device to another: Android phones and tablets, Windows PCs and Macs. No account, no cloud upload, no link.

• Each device has a Contact ID. Share yours once, add the people you send to, and tap a person to send files.
• Files go straight from device to device, end-to-end encrypted. Where no direct path can be found, they pass through a relay, still encrypted.
• A transfer survives a lost connection and a restart, and continues where it stopped. Each item is checked when it arrives.
• Folders are packed, sent and unpacked on the other side.
• Received items go to Download/Dodavo, and nothing is overwritten.
• Share from another app to Dodavo.
• Receive in the background if you like, with a notification that shows that Dodavo is ready.
• Block anyone who sends you things you do not want.
• Made for TalkBack: every control has a name, and what changes is read out.
• In English, Croatian, Serbian (Latin) and Slovenian.

## Croatian (hr)

**Kratki opis:**
Šaljite datoteke i mape izravno na drugi uređaj, sveobuhvatno šifrirano.

**Puni opis:**

Dodavo šalje datoteke i mape izravno s jednog uređaja na drugi: Android telefone i tablete, računala s Windowsima i Mac računala. Bez računa, bez prijenosa u oblak, bez poveznica.

• Svaki uređaj ima ID kontakta. Podijelite svoj jednom, dodajte osobe kojima šaljete i dodirnite osobu da biste poslali datoteke.

• Prijenos preživljava gubitak veze i ponovno pokretanje te se nastavlja ondje gdje je stao. Svaka se stavka provjerava kada stigne.
• Mape se pakiraju, šalju i na drugoj strani raspakiraju.
• Primljene stavke idu u Download/Dodavo i ništa se ne prebrisuje.
• Dijelite iz druge aplikacije u Dodavo.
• Ako želite, primajte u pozadini, uz obavijest koja pokazuje da je Dodavo spreman.
• Blokirajte svakoga tko vam šalje ono što ne želite.
• Izrađen za TalkBack: svaka kontrola ima naziv, a ono što se mijenja čita se naglas.
