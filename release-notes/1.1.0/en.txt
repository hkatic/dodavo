Changes in 1.1.0

- New: Size limit. In Settings you can set the largest item that someone can send you, for everyone or for each recipient separately, and whether Dodavo asks about a larger one or declines it.
- New: Change your Contact ID. In Settings you can get a new Contact ID, and the old one stops working.
- New: Send to several recipients at once. On Windows, check the boxes next to the recipients; on a Mac, select several of them in the list.
- New: Block senders. Block someone with "Block Sender" when their offer arrives, or a recipient in your list with Ctrl+B (Command-B on a Mac). Dodavo then declines everything that person sends, and they are not told. You can unblock them in Settings.
- New: Folder for temporary files. You can choose it in Settings, and Dodavo warns you in advance if a temporary file would be too large for a FAT32 drive.
- New: Windows: the progress window can close by itself when a transfer ends. Check "Close this window when the transfer ends" in any progress window, or turn it on in Settings.
- New: Chat. You can write a message to a person in your recipient list: select them and press Ctrl+M (Shift-Command-M on a Mac). One message can go to several people at once, and in Settings, under Chat, you choose who can write to you. On Windows you can select, copy and delete messages in the chat window, and the main window has a Chat button. The other side needs Dodavo 1.1.0 too.
- New: Sounds. Dodavo plays a sound when something arrives, when a transfer starts, completes or fails, and when the connection is lost. In Settings, under Sounds, you can replace each sound with a sound file of your own (a WAV file on Windows), or turn it off.
- Changed: Windows: when a transfer ends, its progress window stays and shows the result, with buttons that open a received file or folder, in place of the message after a send and the question after a receive.
- Changed: A received folder is unpacked on the drive you save it to, so it no longer needs room on the system drive.
- Changed: Windows: Settings are arranged on tabs.
- Bugfix: At startup, Dodavo no longer says that it is offline while it is still connecting.
- Bugfix: A question that is open in one transfer's window no longer holds up the other transfers.
- Bugfix: An item that starts by itself is checked for free space together with the transfers that are still arriving, where several large items could fill the disk before.
- Bugfix: Error messages about a connection, a check for updates and the network no longer appear in English in the other languages.
- Bugfix: Windows: an item with a very long name is received with a shortened name, where it failed before.
- Bugfix: Windows: when a received folder has two files whose names differ only in upper and lower case, both are kept, where one replaced the other before.
- Bugfix: Windows: the tooltip of the icon in the notification area no longer shows a speed of 0 B/s once all data has been sent.
- Bugfix: Windows: Check for Updates no longer opens a second message while a check is already running.
- Bugfix: Windows: a received file that another program changed while its transfer was interrupted is no longer kept as complete; the transfer fails instead.
- Bugfix: Windows: the progress window of a completed transfer shows the whole size, where it could keep what it showed at the start.
- Bugfix: Mac: Dodavo says so when the recipient of a send is no longer in the list.
- Bugfix: Mac: several items from one person are offered in one window with one answer, where each opened a window of its own on top of the others.
